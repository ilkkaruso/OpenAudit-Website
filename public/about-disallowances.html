What Are Disallowances?

Disallowances are government expenditures that the Commission on Audit (COA) has determined to be irregular, unnecessary, excessive, extravagant, or unconscionable. These represent funds that were spent inappropriately or without proper documentation, and officials may be required to return these amounts to the government.

This interactive map visualizes the total disallowances per province from 2016 to 2022, providing insights into financial governance and accountability across the Philippines.

Risk Level Calculation

The risk levels are calculated using a logarithmic scale based on the total disallowance amounts. This approach helps normalize the wide range of values and provides a meaningful comparison between provinces.

Data Source & Methodology

All data is sourced from official Commission on Audit (COA) Annual Audit Reports covering the period 2016-2022. The dataset includes:

  • Province-level aggregated disallowances
  • Individual LGU (Local Government Unit) disallowance records
  • Notice of Disallowance (ND) ending balances
  • Year-by-year tracking of audit findings
Note: Some provinces may show no data due to either having zero disallowances during the period or incomplete data availability in the source reports. The ARMM/BARMM special administrative region can be highlighted separately using the toggle option.

Special Features

  • Interactive Visualization: Hover over provinces to see detailed information
  • Risk-Based Coloring: Quick visual identification of high-risk areas
  • ARMM/BARMM Overlay: Special highlighting for autonomous regions
  • Comprehensive Coverage: Data from 87 provinces and 1,751 LGUs

Understanding the Colors

The color intensity on the map directly correlates with the severity of disallowances:

  • Dark Red: Provinces with the highest disallowances (often major urban centers)
  • Red to Orange: Significant disallowances requiring attention
  • Yellow to Green: Lower levels of disallowances
  • Gray: No data available or zero disallowances recorded
