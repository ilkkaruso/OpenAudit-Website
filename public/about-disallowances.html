OpenAudit Philippines

Promoting Transparency Through Data-Driven Accountability

OpenAudit is an innovative platform that visualizes Commission on Audit (COA) disallowances across Philippine Local Government Units (LGUs), providing citizens with unprecedented access to public financial accountability data. By normalizing disallowances against total operating income, we reveal the true scale of fiscal irregularities across provinces and municipalities.

Understanding the Disallowance Ratio

Traditional reporting of disallowances in absolute peso amounts fails to account for the vast differences in economic scale between LGUs. A ₱10 million disallowance means something very different for a small municipality versus a highly urbanized city.

Our Solution: The Disallowance Ratio
Disallowance Ratio = (Total Disallowances ÷ Total Operating Income) × 100

This metric normalizes disallowances as a percentage of an LGU's total operating income, providing a fair comparison across all economic scales.

By using Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE) data from the Department of Finance, we calculate what percentage of an LGU's operating budget is tied up in questioned expenditures. This approach reveals patterns that absolute numbers obscure.

Interpreting the Heat Map

Our color-coded visualization uses a gradient from yellow (low ratios) to dark red (high ratios), making it immediately apparent which areas have concerning levels of disallowances relative to their economic capacity.

Why This Matters

Exposing Systemic Issues

Disallowances represent government expenditures that violate laws, rules, or regulations. While some may be technical violations, patterns of high disallowance ratios often indicate:

  • Weak internal controls and financial management systems
  • Potential corruption or misuse of public funds
  • Lack of compliance with procurement laws and regulations
  • Governance challenges that may correlate with political dynasties

Empowering Citizens

OpenAudit democratizes access to complex audit data, transforming thousands of pages of COA reports into an intuitive visual interface. Citizens can now:

  • Compare their LGU's performance against regional and national benchmarks
  • Track improvements or deterioration over multiple years
  • Hold local officials accountable with concrete, normalized metrics
  • Make informed decisions during elections based on fiscal responsibility
Research Connection: Studies have shown correlations between areas with entrenched political dynasties and higher rates of fiscal irregularities. By making this data accessible and comparable, OpenAudit enables researchers and journalists to investigate these patterns systematically.

Our database integrates COA Annual Audit Reports with Department of Finance SRE data, creating the most comprehensive public accountability dataset available for the Philippines. The platform updates as new audit reports are released, ensuring citizens have access to the latest accountability information.

Technical Implementation

OpenAudit leverages modern web technologies to deliver a responsive, interactive experience:

  • Data Processing: Python scripts normalize and match COA disallowances with DOF financial data
  • Visualization: Leaflet.js renders interactive maps with GeoJSON boundaries
  • User Interface: Click-based interaction reveals detailed breakdowns for each area
  • Open Source: All code and processed data available for researchers and developers

The platform calculates ratios dynamically, showing both the total disallowances (numerator) and total operating income (denominator) for full transparency in our methodology.

OpenAudit Philippines is a civic technology initiative promoting transparency and accountability in local governance.

Data sources: Commission on Audit (COA) Annual Audit Reports | Department of Finance Statement of Receipts and Expenditures
