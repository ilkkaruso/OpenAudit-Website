OpenAudit Philippines

Transparency Through Data: Making Public Accountability Accessible

OpenAudit transforms complex Commission on Audit (COA) reports into an intuitive, interactive platform that reveals patterns of fiscal management across all Philippine Local Government Units. By calculating disallowance ratios—the percentage of questioned expenditures relative to total operating income—we provide citizens with a clear, comparable metric for understanding how public funds are managed.

The Disallowance Ratio: A Normalized Metric

Raw disallowance amounts fail to capture the true scale of fiscal irregularities. A ₱10 million disallowance represents vastly different levels of concern for a small municipality versus a highly urbanized city. Our solution normalizes these values to enable meaningful comparisons.

The Calculation:
Disallowance Ratio = (Total Disallowances ÷ Total Operating Income) × 100

This ratio expresses disallowances as a percentage of an LGU's total operating income, revealing the proportion of public resources subject to audit exceptions.

Data Sources and Methodology

We combine two authoritative datasets:

  • COA Annual Audit Reports (2016-2022): Comprehensive disallowance records for all LGUs
  • Department of Finance Statement of Receipts and Expenditures: Total Current Operating Income data for each LGU

For each LGU, we calculate the average operating income across available years and compare it against total disallowances for the same period. This approach accounts for year-to-year variations and provides a stable baseline for comparison.

Complete Coverage: Our enhanced dataset now includes Total Operating Income for 1,176 municipalities and cities, enabling true ratio calculations for 822 LGUs with recorded disallowances—a significant improvement from previous estimates.

Transparency and Accountability

What Disallowances Reveal

Disallowances occur when government expenditures violate laws, rules, or regulations. Patterns of high disallowance ratios often indicate:

  • Weak internal controls and inadequate financial management systems
  • Non-compliance with procurement laws and government accounting standards
  • Irregular, unnecessary, excessive, or extravagant expenditures
  • Transactions lacking proper documentation or authorization
  • Potential corruption or misuse of public funds

Empowering Citizens Through Data

OpenAudit democratizes access to public financial data by:

  • Simplifying Complexity: Converting technical audit reports into visual, intuitive metrics
  • Enabling Comparisons: Normalizing data across LGUs of different sizes and budgets
  • Tracking Progress: Showing changes over multiple years (2016-2022)
  • Promoting Accountability: Making fiscal performance transparent and accessible
  • Supporting Research: Providing data for journalists, academics, and civil society
Informed Citizenship: By making this data accessible, we enable citizens to ask informed questions about public spending, hold officials accountable for fiscal management, and make evidence-based decisions during elections.

Beyond Individual LGUs

The aggregated data reveals broader patterns that merit investigation:

  • Regional variations in fiscal management practices
  • Correlation between disallowance ratios and development indicators
  • Temporal trends showing improvement or deterioration
  • Clustering of high-ratio areas that may indicate systemic issues

Technical Implementation

Open Source and Transparent

OpenAudit is built on principles of transparency and reproducibility:

  • Data Processing: Python scripts normalize and match COA disallowances with DOF income data
  • Visualization: Interactive Leaflet.js maps with GeoJSON boundaries for all LGUs
  • Real-time Calculation: Ratios computed dynamically, showing both numerator and denominator
  • Open Access: All code and processed datasets available for verification and reuse

Data Quality and Limitations

We acknowledge several limitations in the current dataset:

  • Some LGUs lack complete data across all years
  • Matching between different government databases can be imperfect due to naming inconsistencies
  • Disallowances may be subsequently cleared or appealed, which is not reflected in annual totals
  • The ratio alone doesn't distinguish between technical violations and serious irregularities

Despite these limitations, the disallowance ratio provides a valuable starting point for understanding fiscal management patterns and identifying areas that warrant closer examination.
